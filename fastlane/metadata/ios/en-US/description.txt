NepDate is a light, fast Nepali patro. It answers what the Bikram Sambat date is, whether today is a public holiday, and what is coming, without ads, accounts or a network.

- The month grid with big BS numerals, small AD dates, holidays and Saturdays in red, the tithi and every festival.
- The today card with the day, the weekday, the tithi and the next public holiday.
- The festivals list and a search for names in English or Nepali and for a typed BS or AD date.
- A converter between Bikram Sambat and Gregorian dates, and the difference between two dates.
- Home Screen widgets for today, the month, the next festival, this week and the festivals list, plus Lock Screen and Control Center items.
- English and Nepali, with the system's per-app language page.
- No ads, no analytics, no account, and no network calls.
