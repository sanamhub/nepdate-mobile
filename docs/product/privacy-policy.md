# Privacy policy

Effective from the first public release. This text is published at the URL given to the App Store
and Google Play (GitHub Pages of this repository) and shown in the app under Settings → About.

NepDate (the app) does not collect, store, send or share any personal data.

- It has no network access. On Android it does not even request the internet permission.
- It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking.
- Your settings (language, digits, time zone choice, tithi display) stay on your device. They are
  shared only with the app's own widgets on the same device.
- "Add to calendar" opens your phone's calendar app with the event filled in. You decide whether to
  save it; NepDate never reads your calendar.
- "Subscribe to Nepal holidays" hands a public calendar address to your calendar app. Your calendar
  app downloads that file; NepDate does not.
- Crash and diagnostic reports, if you have allowed them in your phone's settings, go to Apple or
  Google under their policies, not to us.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/sanamhub/nepdate-mobile.
